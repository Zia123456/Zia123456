## 🚀 About Me

I am an interdisciplinary **Civil Engineer, Urban Planner, and Full-Stack Software Engineer** interested in using computational technologies to solve complex problems in the **built environment**.

My work sits at the intersection of:

**🏗️ Construction & Civil Engineering**  
**🏙️ Urban & Regional Planning**  
**🗺️ GIS & Location Intelligence**  
**🏢 BIM & Digital Construction**  
**🤖 Artificial Intelligence & Computer Vision**  
**💻 Full-Stack Software Engineering**

I am particularly interested in **Construction Informatics and Digital Construction Management**, where engineering knowledge can be combined with software, AI, BIM, geospatial data, and real-time project information to improve the way construction projects are planned, monitored, and managed.

> **My goal is to bridge the gap between the physical built environment and intelligent digital systems.**

## 🦺 Site Safety Guard AI

**AI-assisted construction site safety monitoring system**

A conceptual computer-vision system designed to analyze construction sites using drone or camera imagery and identify potential safety hazards.

### Potential detection targets

- ⛑️ Missing PPE / helmets
- 🚧 Unprotected edges
- 🕳️ Unsafe trenches
- 💧 Wet/slippery areas
- 🔌 Loose electrical cables
- 🧰 Misplaced tools and materials
- 👷 Unsafe worker–equipment interactions

🔗 **Repository:**  
https://github.com/Zia123456/site_safety_guard_ai
